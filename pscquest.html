Project Name

KERALA PSC QUIZ & PRACTICE PLATFORM

Description

PSC Quest is a functional MVP designed and developed to help Kerala PSC aspirants practise through a clear, focused digital experience. The current release brings the essential preparation journey into one platform through practice quizzes, previous-year questions, and timed mock tests.

As an MVP, the project prioritizes the core experience needed to validate the product idea and support real use. Its simple dashboard and direct navigation reduce friction between choosing a practice mode and starting a session, while leaving room for future improvements guided by user feedback and learning needs.
